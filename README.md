# KII communication review

## Why this exists: the point

*[O] My own view (stp), based on the evidence below.*

KII began announcing and promising products before the tools to build them existed. Its public product claims came about **3 to 21 months before covenants went live on Kaspa mainnet** ([DELIVERY.md](DELIVERY.md#claims-dated-against-the-kaspa-protocol)). Crescendo (May 2025) brought about 10 blocks per second, not programmability. Covenants and programmability arrived with **Toccata on 30 Jun 2026**. Back then nobody knew whether Kaspa would deliver. On the core it now does, credit to the Kaspa core developers. Promising products on tools that do not yet exist is, in my judgement, not serious.

The pattern: KII puts the Kaspa name in front of industry and institutions, but I can find no audited, verifiable product. Its communication is poor: an April 2025 satellite announcement was still the pinned post in October 2026; a post was deleted after it was publicly questioned; website wording about product status was rewritten without notice; and I was blocked after replying ([WHAT-HAPPENED.md](WHAT-HAPPENED.md), [CHRONOLOGY.md](CHRONOLOGY.md), [COMMUNICATION.md](COMMUNICATION.md), [EVIDENCE.md](EVIDENCE.md)).

This is not how a professional organisation operates. If I were a future customer or partner, I would look at this record and think twice; on the current record, I would not engage with KII.

Perhaps real work is happening under NDAs with partners who have concrete goals. I hope so. But in public only the record can be judged, and it has cost KII credibility.

That is the goal of this repo: to make that point with evidence, not to dump documents or to break KII. What would change my view: audited products, verifiable mainnet transactions, open code or specifications, and honest corrections.

## What

The goal is to make one thing visible: KII's **repeated pattern of poor communication and misfiring strategic announcements**. Things are announced as launched, operational, completed or "ready". Then they cannot be used or checked, are quietly dropped, or are later rewritten without explanation.

One post or one edit proves little. **The pattern is the finding.**

## Recommendation: keep Kaspa out of this initiative until it delivers [O]

**The observed record:**
- KII presents **Kaspa** to industry, energy and institutional audiences: Dii summits, financial institutions and trade-finance programmes. It does so under a name that puts Kaspa first.
- Across two years, the public record shows **announcements that outran delivery**: launches without public access, test claims without artefacts, a satellite with a launch window and no hardware, "ready" products that are closed and unaudited.
- It also shows **content that disappeared** shortly after it was publicly examined. That is timing only; no cause is claimed.

**The effect:** for audiences who meet Kaspa through KII, that record now **reflects on Kaspa itself**. Kaspa's actual engineering (Crescendo, Toccata, covenants on mainnet) is verifiable on chain. KII's product claims mostly are not. Presenting the second under the name of the first damages how Kaspa is perceived.

**In stp's words:**

> KII says it is an independent non-profit and not a product developer, yet the Kaspa name sits in front of industry and institutions, so its unverified claims reflect on Kaspa. KII can bring Kaspa back into the name once its products are audited and verifiable on mainnet.

**Recommendation:**
- KII should **drop "Kaspa" from its name and positioning** (for example "Kii Foundation", a name it already uses for its foundation) until its products are **audited and verifiable on Kaspa mainnet**: public transaction IDs, a published audit, and a named pilot partner who confirms it.
- Until then, every KII presentation should carry a clear line: *"Independent initiative. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Kaspa core development."*

This is a recommendation based on the public record above. It is not an allegation of bad faith. KII is entitled to build closed products, run private demos and change its website. The issue is **attaching Kaspa's name** to claims that the public cannot verify.

## Fairness [F]

- **The covenant is real.** KII's self-replicating "quine" covenant was accepted on Kaspa mainnet on 30 Jun 2026 (genesis transaction `1c1a4c549c664147814d9836e623373991622c1dc711a4227f206ad5f6a241c5`, 18:21:42 CEST), the day Toccata activated. Whether it was the *first* covenant on mainnet is not established.
- **Public research exists and is honestly labelled:**
  - [Portrait](https://github.com/KaspaKii/Portrait) (MIT, "Pre-production · unaudited · testnet-only");
  - [kaspa-pq-zk-bench](https://github.com/KaspaKii/kaspa-pq-zk-bench) (post-quantum signatures in a zkVM; benchmarked by a third party);
  - [optical-mining-kaspa](https://github.com/KaspaKii/optical-mining-kaspa), a study that concludes against its own idea (uncompetitive by 30–80x).
- **Recent website corrections**, between 3 Oct 20:55 and 5 Oct 00:09 CEST:
  - "Built, tested, ready for launch" was removed;
  - the foundation now says it "does not develop, sell, or operate" the products;
  - the products are now attributed to "KiiLabs".
  - These go in the direction this review asked for. The new line "KiiWORKS anchors on Kaspa mainnet today" still has no published transaction ID, and KiiLabs is not yet identified (no legal entity found).
- **No retail fundraising found:** in 268 original @KaspaKii posts and on the website there is no token sale, presale, donation address or NFT mint.
- **Building early is normal.** Work on testnets before a hard fork is useful. The criticism is about how products were *described in public* (launched, operational, ready), not that work started early.

## Advice for KII [O]

1. **Publish one dated status page** listing every product and initiative as live (with txids), testnet, in development, paused or stopped. Retire KONI, GigaWatt and ZET-EX openly if they are stopped.
2. **Prove mainnet claims with transaction IDs**, and audit claims with a published audit.
3. **Name the people and entities:** board, KvK number, KiiLabs' legal name, owners and jurisdiction, and roles held at Dii and ZETA.
4. **Name pilots only with the partner's consent and confirmation**, otherwise do not mention them.
5. **Keep a public changelog** for website edits, so corrections read as corrections.
6. **Drop "Kaspa" from the name** until the products are verifiable on mainnet (see the recommendation above).
