# Communication findings

Checked 3–5 Oct 2026. Labels: [F] fact, [C] KII claim, [O] opinion.

## 1. Announcements that outran delivery [F]

These are listed in [DELIVERY.md](DELIVERY.md). The communication pattern behind them:
- **Launch language without access.** Examples: "Launching Today" (WarpCore sandbox, 5 Jan 2026); "Completed our main flagship projects" (30 Apr 2026); "Built, tested, ready for launch" (website until 3–5 Oct 2026). None came with a public URL, a test artefact or a named participant.
- **Test results without artefacts:** "286 transaction type tests, 100% pass"; Fed/SEPA "all tests passed".
- **Promised follow-ups that did not come.** KONI: "more detailed project information early next week" (17 May 2025). Nothing followed.
- **Projects dropped silently:** KONI, the GigaWatt stablecoin, ZET-EX, KRC-20T and Kii Academy funding. None was formally retired.
- **Institutional name-dropping that did not hold:** "Officially applied to join the ECB's Pontes Market Contact Group" (30 Aug 2025). KII is not on the ECB's participant list (Oct 2025).

## 3. Transparency went backwards [F]

- **2024:** the team page named the chairman, the secretary general, board members and staff ([Wayback](https://web.archive.org/web/20241117000000*/kaspa-kii.org/about-us/)).
- **2026:** the site names no one. It publishes no Chamber of Commerce (KvK) number, statutes, accounts or funders, and gives no owners for the operating company.
- **Most of what the public knows** about KII's relationships comes from **third parties**:
  - **Dii's white paper with HiTHIUM** (Sep 2026, §7) says ZETA "began research in June 2026 into a DLT-powered certificate registry pilot with the Kaspa Industrial Initiative (Kii)". It credits a KII board member as a contributor "(Kaspa industrial initiative)". [PDF on dii-desertenergy.org](https://dii-desertenergy.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/MENA-BESS-low-res-1.pdf).
  - **ZETA's own site** lists Paul van Son as chairman and Rory O'Neill as secretary general. Both are on KII's 2024 team page.
  - **Dii's summit agendas**, trade press and community accounts.
- **KII said none of this itself** on X or its website, as far as full-archive searches show. **[O]** That is a disclosure gap, not evidence of wrongdoing. But where people hold roles on several sides, silence leaves others to describe the relationship.

## 4. The most concrete thing was never shown [F]

- A KII **"Trusted Trade"** demo existed at `anchorage.kaspa-kii.org` from about 12 Aug 2026 (first TLS certificate): a testnet trade-finance workflow with synthetic data, a verifier and a 2-page brief.
- It was **never announced** on X or linked from KII's website. It was found only through certificate-transparency logs on 5 Oct.
- Within hours of being documented, it was **put behind a password**, together with the KiiWORKS Workbench ([CHRONOLOGY.md](CHRONOLOGY.md) #5–6).
- **[O]** It is honestly labelled (pre-production, unaudited, testnet, synthetic). It is also the closest thing to a working industrial demo that KII has produced. Hiding it while posting "ready for launch" on the website is the pattern in miniature: **the claims are public, the evidence is not.**

## 5. The 3 Oct 2026 Istanbul video post [F]

- The post shared about 30 minutes of audience-filmed footage of **other organisations' presentations** (Dii, YEO Technology, sponsors). KII was not mentioned, shown or represented.
- **Technically it was adequate but unedited:**
  - 1080p video;
  - about 30–40% of the frame is tabletop;
  - distant hall sound at −24 LUFS;
  - no titles, subtitles or speaker names.
- Views fell from 381 to 51 and 68 across the three clips (3 Oct, about 20:05 CEST).
- **The post was deleted by its author** between 3 Oct 22:56 and 4 Oct 08:06 CEST. **This case is closed.** It stands as one example of the missing editorial process.

## 6. Reaction to scrutiny (timing only) [F]

In the 36 hours after the research was published:
- **3 Oct:** the analyst's account was blocked, and the video post was deleted.
- **3–4 Oct:** the pinned KONI post was unpinned.
- **3–5 Oct:** the website was edited.
- **5 Oct:** two demo hosts went behind a password, and the analysis script's user agent was blocked on both the KII and ZETA sites.

There was no public reply, correction notice or status update. See [CHRONOLOGY.md](CHRONOLOGY.md).

**[O]** Each step is within KII's rights. As communication, it **removes rather than explains**. A short public note would have served KII and Kaspa better than silence.
